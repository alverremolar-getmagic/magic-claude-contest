How it works

Contest Timeline

1

Contest Launch on Aug 24

Mechanics, criteria, and prizes release via this page, email, and Discord.

2

Plan, Build, and Test

Think creatively, improve an existing automation, and get it running before submissions open.

3

Submissions open on Aug 31

A write-up and a polished video walkthrough are required. Submissions are open about a week.

4

Judging starts right after close

A panel scores independently against the rubric. We'll compute once all scores are in.

5

Winners announced on Sept 12

You'll receive an email if your workflow is selected. We may use the video to showcase your hardwork.

6

Your workflow keeps working for you

Win or not, you keep the automation and your Claude access.

How you're judged

Weighted Criteria

30%

Business or Client Impact

Telling the story of how your client benefitted from your workflow beats a fancy demo just for show.
30%

Complexity, Creativity, and Mastery

Synergy of connectors, skills, projects, artifacts, or other tools score higher than plain prompting and web search.

25%

Usefulness and Measurable Effects

Quantifiable where possible: time or cost savings, increased KPIs, higher quality deliverables, etc.
15%

Presentation and Application

High quality recording and editing of a walkthrough that other assistants can replicate or be inspired from.

Eligibility

Who can enter?

Active pilot testers and Claude-trained assistants.

Original, individual work. One entry per person.

You may revise and resubmit as long as submissions are open. We'll consider only your last submitted entry as valid for judging.

Prizes

One winner, plus consolation prizes.

$100 for the winning entry.

Starbucks GCs as selected by judges.
